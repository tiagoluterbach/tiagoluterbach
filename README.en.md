## Featured project · Zelo

**Next-day curtailment risk alerts for wind and solar generation.**

Developed as a team project at **Hackathon IA COPPE 2026**, Zelo connects public data from Brazil's national grid operator (ONS) to a dashboard for operational planning. The final prototype identifies risk windows, displays periods without alerts, and provides context based on each plant's history.

I contributed in three areas:

- **Experimentation:** evaluated temporal neural networks (GRU) as an alternative for forecasting curtailed energy volume, comparing performance against existing references. The network did not show consistent gains that justified replacing the evaluated approach.
- **Coordination:** organized responsibilities and alignment across the team to reduce overlapping work.
- **Communication:** developed the final pitch and presentation slides, connecting the problem, solution, and supporting evidence.

In the team's historical evaluation for **September 1–24, 2026**, alert precision was **82% for wind and 81% for solar**, evaluated per plant and 30-minute interval. The final product focuses on occurrence alerts; volume forecasting was part of the experimental work.
